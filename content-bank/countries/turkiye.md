## Overview

Türkiye straddles Europe and Asia at the Bosphorus — 783,562 km² (packaged) and a 2025 population of 85,878,556 (World Bank, packaged). The capital is Ankara; the largest city, Istanbul, is the country's packaged city in this bank. The currency is the Turkish lira (TRY); the language is Turkish; the calling code is +90.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$1.597 trillion, growth 3.60%, GDP per capita US$18,599, unemployment 8.52% — and inflation of 34.88%, by far the highest in this bank's country set, which is the essential context for any price a visitor or a remittance sender meets in Türkiye. Life expectancy was 77.4 years in the packaged indicators.

## Provinces intro

Türkiye is administered in 81 provinces (packaged), from Adana to Zonguldak — a unitary structure in which Istanbul province alone holds a population larger than most of the countries in this bank. The provinces span the Aegean and Mediterranean coasts, the Anatolian plateau and the Black Sea mountains; Ankara province holds the capital, and Istanbul province the commercial metropolis.

The practical frame is variety: four-season climate zones within one country, a strong domestic transport network, and lira pricing that moves with the high inflation recorded above — the live rates layer, not any printed figure, is the reference for money questions.
