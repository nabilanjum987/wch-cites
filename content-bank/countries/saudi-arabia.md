## Overview

Saudi Arabia is the largest country of the Arabian Peninsula — 2,250,000 km² (packaged) — with a 2025 population of 36,973,555 (World Bank, packaged). The capital is Riyadh. The currency is the Saudi riyal (SAR); the language is Arabic; the calling code is +966. The Kingdom holds Islam's two holiest cities, Makkah and Madinah, in its western Hijaz region, and its two packaged cities in this bank — Riyadh and Jeddah — are its capital and its Red Sea gateway.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$1.277 trillion, growth 4.50%, GDP per capita US$34,537, inflation 2.04%, unemployment 3.04%. Life expectancy was 79.0 years and adult literacy 97.9% in the packaged indicators. The economy's foundation is hydrocarbons; its stated direction is diversification, with Riyadh as the administrative centre of that programme and Jeddah as the western commercial and pilgrimage gateway.

## Provinces intro

Saudi Arabia is administered in thirteen regions (packaged): Riyadh, Makkah, Al Madinah, the Eastern Province, Asir, Tabuk, Ha'il, the Northern Borders, Jazan, Najran, Al Bahah, Al Jawf and Qassim. The Eastern Province, on the Gulf, holds the core oil fields; the Makkah and Madinah regions hold the holy cities; Asir, in the south-west, is the Kingdom's mountainous summer retreat.

## Practical & visitor prose

The practical frame is climate and calendar: desert heat across most of the country for much of the year, a milder Hijaz coast at Jeddah, and the pilgrimage seasons that move millions through the western region. Packaged essentials: currency SAR, language Arabic, calling code +966. The package's personalities pool: Ibn Saud, the founding king; King Faisal; King Salman; Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman; filmmaker Haifa al-Mansour; and singer Mohammed Abdu.
