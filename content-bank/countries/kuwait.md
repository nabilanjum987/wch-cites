## Overview

Kuwait is a small Gulf state at the head of the Arabian Gulf — 17,818 km² (packaged) with a 2025 population of 4,865,298 (World Bank, packaged). The capital is Kuwait City, the packaged city in this bank. The currency is the Kuwaiti dinar (KWD); the language is Arabic; the calling code is +965.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$157.2 billion, growth 2.73%, GDP per capita US$32,312, inflation 2.36%, unemployment 2.18%. Life expectancy was 84.6 years — the highest figure in this bank's entire country set. The economy is hydrocarbon-founded, with the state and its sovereign funds at its centre, and the dinar's strength a standing fact of the country's financial identity (this file states no exchange rate — rates belong to the live layer).

## Provinces intro

Kuwait is administered in six governorates (packaged): Al Asimah (the Capital), Hawalli, Al Farwaniya, Mubarak Al-Kabeer, Al Ahmadi and Al Jahra. Al Ahmadi, to the south, holds the oil industry's heartland; Al Jahra reaches toward the Iraqi border; the capital governorate holds Kuwait City itself.
