## Overview

The United States is a federation of fifty states plus the District of Columbia — 9,826,675 km² (packaged) and a 2025 population of 341,784,857 (World Bank, packaged). The capital is Washington, D.C.; the largest city, New York, is the packaged city in this bank. The currency is the US dollar (USD); the calling code is +1; the packaged language list is headed by English, with Hawaiian, Spanish, Samoan, Chamorro and Carolinian among the recognised languages in the country data.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$30.77 trillion — the largest economy in the world and in this bank — growth 2.16%, GDP per capita US$90,027 (the highest in this set), unemployment 4.20%, inflation 2.95% (2024). Life expectancy was 78.9 years in the packaged indicators. The dollar's role as the world's reserve currency is the frame for much of this bank's finance content: gold, oil and the Fear & Greed sentiment layer all price off US markets, and the USD→PKR pair is the anchor corridor of the remittance evergreen.

## Provinces intro

The packaged subdivision list carries 51 entries — the fifty states plus the District of Columbia — from Alabama to Wyoming. The states vary in population from under a million to California's near-forty million (a state-level figure this file does not itemise, as the package does not carry state populations), and each keeps its own legislature, taxes and character inside the federation.

## Practical & visitor prose

The practical frame is continental scale: six time zones in the fifty states, climates from arctic Alaska to tropical Hawaii, and domestic air travel as the default long-distance mode. Packaged essentials: currency USD, calling code +1, emergency 911 (as packaged in the New York city file). The package's personalities pool: George Washington, Abraham Lincoln, Martin Luther King Jr., Neil Armstrong, Oprah Winfrey and Barack Obama.
