## Overview

Qatar is a peninsular state on the Gulf's western shore — 11,437 km² (packaged) with a 2025 population of 2,972,215 (World Bank, packaged), the smallest population in this bank's Gulf set. The capital is Doha, the packaged city in this bank. The currency is the Qatari riyal (QAR); the language is Arabic; the calling code is +974.

The economy is liquefied natural gas at world scale, administered from Doha, with the state's investment and aviation sectors layered on top.

## Provinces intro

Qatar is administered in eight municipalities (packaged): Ad Dawhah (Doha), Al Rayyan, Al Wakrah, Al Khor and Al Thakhira, Al Daayen, Al Shamal, Al-Shahaniya and Umm Salal. Al Rayyan, adjoining the capital, is the largest by area of the urban municipalities; Al Shamal covers the northern tip; the population's centre of gravity is the Doha–Al Rayyan conurbation.
