## Overview

India is the world's most populous country — 1,463,865,525 people in 2025 (World Bank, packaged) — and its seventh largest by area at 3,287,263 km² (packaged). The capital is New Delhi. The currency is the Indian rupee (INR); Hindi and English are the packaged official languages, among many; the calling code is +91.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$3.956 trillion — the largest economy in this bank after the United States — growth 7.57%, the fastest in the set, GDP per capita US$2,702, inflation 2.40%, unemployment 4.22%, and a labour force of 617.6 million. Life expectancy was 72.2 years (2024) and adult literacy 78.2% (2024). Personal remittances received were US$150.7 billion (2025) — the largest remittance inflow in this bank and, as the World Bank series records, the world's largest, a fact that anchors the Pakistan–India comparison and the remittance evergreen elsewhere in this bank.

## Provinces intro

India is a union of 28 states and 8 union territories — 36 subdivisions in the packaged list, from Andhra Pradesh to West Bengal, including the National Capital Territory of Delhi. The packaged subdivision source is the standard states-and-territories list; the states differ enormously in population, language and economy, which is why the country file treats them as a federation of near-countries rather than as provinces of a single mould.

## Practical & visitor prose

India's practical frame is scale: multiple climatic zones, a rail network that is a travel system in itself, and regional languages at every border. Packaged essentials: currency INR, languages Hindi and English (with state languages throughout), calling code +91. The package's personalities pool spans the country's self-image: Mahatma Gandhi, Jawaharlal Nehru, A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, Sachin Tendulkar, Lata Mangeshkar and Shah Rukh Khan.
