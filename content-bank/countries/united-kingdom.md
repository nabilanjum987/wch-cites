## Overview

The United Kingdom is a union of four constituent countries — England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland (packaged) — covering 242,495 km² with a 2025 population of 69,487,000 (World Bank, packaged). The capital is London, also the packaged city in this bank. The currency is the pound sterling (GBP); the calling code is +44; the packaged language list is headed by English, with Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Scots, Cornish, Irish, Ulster Scots and British Sign Language all recognised in the country data.

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$4.003 trillion, growth 1.39%, GDP per capita US$57,602, inflation 3.88%, unemployment 4.75%. Life expectancy was 81.4 years in the packaged indicators. The economy is services-led, with London — banked in B1 — as its financial centre; the UK is also one of the principal remittance-sending countries for the Pakistani diaspora, which is why the GBP→PKR pair is one of the four corridors in this bank's remittance evergreen.

## Provinces intro

The UK's subdivisions are its four constituent countries (packaged): England, the largest by population, holding London; Scotland, with its own parliament, legal system and Edinburgh capital; Wales, with Welsh-language institutions and Cardiff; and Northern Ireland, on the island of Ireland, with Belfast. Below that level England divides into counties and the devolved nations into council areas — the packaged set stops at the constituent-country level, and so does this file.
