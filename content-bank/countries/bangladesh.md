## Overview

Bangladesh is a delta nation at the head of the Bay of Bengal, formed by the Ganges, Brahmaputra and Meghna river systems. Its capital is Dhaka. The World Bank put its 2025 population at 175,686,899, on a land area of 147,570 km² (packaged) — one of the most densely populated large countries in the world. The currency is the Bangladeshi taka (BDT); the language is Bangla; the calling code is +880.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$456.3 billion, growth 3.49%, GDP per capita US$2,597, inflation 8.77%, unemployment 3.78%, and a labour force of 74.7 million. Life expectancy was 74.9 years (2024) and adult literacy 79% (2022). Personal remittances received were US$33.9 billion (2025) — one of the economy's defining inflows, earned substantially by Bangladeshi workers in the Gulf, which is why this country's diaspora pairs matter to the rates and remittance content elsewhere in this bank.

## Provinces intro

Bangladesh is administered in eight divisions (packaged): Dhaka, Chattogram, Khulna, Rajshahi, Barishal, Sylhet, Rangpur and Mymensingh, each named for its principal city. Dhaka division holds the capital and the country's demographic weight; Chattogram is the port division on the Bay of Bengal; Sylhet, in the north-east, is the division most associated with the country's overseas diaspora.

## Practical & visitor prose

Bangladesh's practical frame is the monsoon: a hot, wet season that floods the delta productively, and a mild, dry winter that is the natural visiting season. The packaged country data gives the essentials — currency BDT, language Bangla, calling code +880.
