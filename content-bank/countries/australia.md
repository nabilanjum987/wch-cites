## Overview

Australia is a continent-country — 7,692,024 km² (packaged), the largest landmass in this bank — with a 2025 population of 27,614,411 (World Bank, packaged) concentrated on its coasts. The capital is Canberra; the largest city is Sydney. Three Australian cities are packaged in this bank: Sydney, Brisbane and Canberra. The currency is the Australian dollar (AUD); the calling code is +61; the packaged language list is headed by Australian English and Auslan.

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$1.799 trillion, growth 1.35%, GDP per capita US$65,130, inflation 2.87%, unemployment 4.09%. ## Provinces intro

Australia is a federation of six states and two mainland territories — eight subdivisions in the packaged list: New South Wales, Victoria, Queensland, Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania, the Australian Capital Territory and the Northern Territory. Sydney is in New South Wales, Brisbane in Queensland, and Canberra inside the ACT — the three banked cities conveniently sit in three different subdivisions.
