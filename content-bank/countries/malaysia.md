## Overview

Malaysia is a two-part federation — Peninsular Malaysia and Malaysian Borneo — covering 330,803 km² (packaged) with a 2025 population of 35,977,838 (World Bank, packaged). The capital is Kuala Lumpur; George Town, the Penang capital, is the second Malaysian city in this bank. The currency is the Malaysian ringgit (MYR); the packaged language is Malay; the calling code is +60.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$472.2 billion, growth 5.17% — the second-fastest in this set after India — GDP per capita US$13,125, inflation 1.38%, unemployment 3.76%. Life expectancy was 76.8 years in the packaged indicators. The economy spans electronics manufacturing (with Penang's cluster the emblem), commodities, and services centred on Kuala Lumpur.

## Provinces intro

Malaysia is a federation of thirteen states and three federal territories — sixteen subdivisions in the packaged list: Johor, Kedah, Kelantan, Malacca, Negeri Sembilan, Pahang, Penang, Perak, Perlis, Sabah, Sarawak, Selangor and Terengganu, plus the federal territories. Kuala Lumpur sits inside Selangor's embrace as a federal territory; George Town is the capital of Penang, the island-and-mainland state in the north-west.
