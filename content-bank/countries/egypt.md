## Overview

Egypt is the Nile country — 1,010,408 km² (packaged), most of it desert, with a 2025 population of 118,365,995 (World Bank, packaged) concentrated along the river and its delta. The capital is Cairo; Alexandria, on the Mediterranean, is the second city — both are packaged in this bank. The currency is the Egyptian pound (EGP); the language is Arabic; the calling code is +20.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$365.3 billion, growth 4.39%, GDP per capita US$3,086, inflation 14.07% — second-highest in this set after Türkiye — unemployment 6.78%. Life expectancy was 71.8 years in the packaged indicators. Personal remittances received were US$41.5 billion (2025), larger than Bangladesh's inflow and earned substantially by Egyptians working in the Gulf — the economic thread that connects Cairo and Alexandria to the Gulf capitals in this bank.

## Provinces intro

Egypt is administered in 27 governorates (packaged), from Cairo, Giza and Alexandria through the delta governorates such as Dakahlia, Beheira and Gharbia to the Red Sea and the canal cities such as Ismailia. The governorate structure is unitary rather than federal: governors are appointed, and the great divide in Egyptian geography is between the Nile valley and delta — where nearly everyone lives — and the desert governorates that hold most of the land.
