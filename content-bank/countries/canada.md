## Overview

Canada is the world's second-largest country by area — 9,984,670 km² (packaged) — with a 2025 population of 41,651,653 (World Bank, packaged) gathered mostly along its southern border. The capital is Ottawa; the largest city is Toronto, and Vancouver is the Pacific gateway — both are packaged in this bank. The currency is the Canadian dollar (CAD); the official languages are English and French; the calling code is +1.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$2.320 trillion, growth 1.74%, GDP per capita US$55,698, inflation 2.07%, unemployment 6.91% — the highest unemployment rate in this bank's set. Life expectancy was 82.1 years in the packaged indicators. The economy is resources, manufacturing and services intertwined with the United States market; Toronto, banked in B1, is its financial centre, and Vancouver its Asia-facing port.

## Provinces intro

Canada is a federation of ten provinces and three territories — thirteen subdivisions in the packaged list: Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, Alberta, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Prince Edward Island, and the territories of Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut. Toronto sits in Ontario, Vancouver in British Columbia — the two banked cities bookend the country.

## Practical & visitor prose

The practical frame is a northern climate and bilingual federal services: winters are the defining season across most of the country, and French is an official language everywhere, dominant in Quebec. Packaged essentials: currency CAD, calling code +1, emergency 911 (as packaged in both Canadian city files). Local sources named in the city packages are Statistics Canada's Web Data Service and the city open-data portals. The package's personalities pool: Pierre Trudeau, Terry Fox, Wayne Gretzky, Leonard Cohen, Margaret Atwood and Celine Dion.
