## Overview

Oman is the Sultanate at the south-eastern corner of Arabia — 309,500 km² (packaged) with a 2025 population of 5,494,691 (World Bank, packaged), fronting the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea, with the exclave of Musandam at the Strait of Hormuz. The capital is Muscat, the packaged city in this bank. The currency is the Omani rial (OMR); the language is Arabic; the calling code is +968.

## Economy narrative

The packaged World Bank snapshot (2025): GDP US$109.6 billion, growth 2.40%, GDP per capita US$19,947, unemployment 3.25% — and inflation of 0.97%, the lowest in this bank's entire country set. Life expectancy was 80.2 years in the packaged indicators. The economy is hydrocarbon-founded with diversification into ports, logistics and tourism; Muscat concentrates its government and finance.

## Provinces intro

Oman is administered in eleven governorates (packaged): Muscat, Dhofar, Musandam, Al Buraimi, Ad Dakhiliyah, Al Batinah North, Al Batinah South, Ash Sharqiyah South, Ash Sharqiyah North, Ad Dhahirah and Al Wusta. Dhofar, in the far south, is the exception to Oman's desert register — the khareef monsoon greens it each summer; Musandam is the separated northern exclave.
