## City intro

Sydney is Australia's largest city — about 5,450,496 people (2023, as packaged from Wikidata) in the Australia/Sydney time zone — built around the harbour that produced its two world symbols: the Sydney Opera House on Bennelong Point and the Sydney Harbour Bridge joining the shores.

The Rocks, the city's oldest quarter, sits under the bridge's southern end; the Royal Botanic Garden wraps the harbour edge from the Opera House; and Bondi Beach carries the city's surf identity to the world. Sydney is not the capital — Canberra is — but it is Australia's financial centre and its front door.
