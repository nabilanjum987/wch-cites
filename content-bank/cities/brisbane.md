## City intro

Brisbane is the capital of Queensland — about 2,706,966 people (2023, as packaged from Wikidata) in the Australia/Brisbane time zone — built on the bends of the Brisbane River. Its symbols are the packaged set: the Story Bridge over the river, the South Bank Parklands on the old Expo site, Lone Pine Koala Sanctuary, the Queensland Gallery of Modern Art, and Mount Coot-tha's lookout above the city.

Brisbane's register is subtropical and outdoor: a river city of verandas, parklands and ferry stops, and the mainland gateway for Moreton Bay. It is a state capital whose growth runs on the Queensland economy — resources, agriculture, education and tourism up the coast.
