## City intro

Sialkot, in north-eastern Punjab, counted 591,668 residents at the 2017 census — a city whose global footprint is far larger than its population. Its workshops hand-stitch a majority of the world's footballs, and its surgical-instrument and sports-goods clusters supply markets on every continent.

It is also a city of poets. Allama Muhammad Iqbal, Pakistan's national poet, was born here — Iqbal Manzil, his birthplace, is preserved as a museum — and Faiz Ahmed Faiz, the other great Urdu poet of the twentieth century, was born in the district. Sialkot Fort and Puran's Well carry its older layers.

At 256 metres in the Punjab plains near the Chenab, Sialkot is one of the export powerhouses of the Golden Triangle of Punjab industry, with Gujranwala and Gujrat.
