## City intro

Vancouver is Canada's Pacific city — about 662,248 people in the city proper (2021 census figure, as packaged from Wikidata), in the America/Vancouver time zone, with a much larger metropolitan region around it. Its setting is its identity: downtown towers against the North Shore mountains, with Stanley Park's rainforest peninsula at the harbour's mouth.

The packaged landmark set maps the visitor's city: Stanley Park and its seawall, Granville Island's markets, the Capilano Suspension Bridge in the North Vancouver canyon, Gastown's historic streets, and the Vancouver Aquarium inside the park. Vancouver's register is outdoor and maritime — a port city that lives facing its water and mountains.

## Economy

The packaged national frame (World Bank): Canada's GDP was US$2.320 trillion in 2025, with GDP per capita of US$55,698, inflation of 2.07% and unemployment of 6.91%. Vancouver is the country's Pacific gateway within that economy — its largest western port and a film, technology and trade hub. The package's local sources are the Statistics Canada Web Data Service and the City of Vancouver Open Data portal. No city-level GDP figure is claimed: the package carries none.

## Living & practical

Vancouver has mild, wet winters and dry, warm summers by Canadian standards; the live weather layer on the city page carries current conditions. The currency is the Canadian dollar (CAD). The packaged food list is Pacific: salmon, poutine, the Japadog, the Nanaimo bar and spot prawns.

Emergency number (packaged): 911 for all emergency services.
