## City intro

Gujranwala, in northern Punjab, counted 2,027,001 residents at the 2017 census. It is the birthplace of Maharaja Ranjit Singh, the founder of the Sikh Empire, and the samadhi of his father Maha Singh and Sheranwala Bagh still anchor its historic core. The shrine of Rori Sahib at nearby Eminabad adds to the district's Sikh heritage.

The city carries two famous identities at once. It is Pakistan's wrestling city — the home of pehlwani, traditional South Asian wrestling, whose akharas made Gujranwala's name across the subcontinent — and a city that takes food seriously enough that its chargha is a national reference point.

At 231 metres on the Grand Trunk Road corridor, Gujranwala is also an industrial city of the northern Punjab manufacturing belt.
