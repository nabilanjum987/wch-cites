## City intro

Peshawar, the capital of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, counted 1,970,042 residents at the 2017 census. It is one of the oldest living cities of South Asia, set in its valley at 359 metres where the routes from Central Asia come down through the Khyber Pass — the gateway role that made it a trading city for two thousand years.

The old city holds that history in layers: the Qissa Khwani Bazaar, the bazaar of storytellers; the Mahabat Khan Mosque; Bala Hissar Fort on its rise; and the Peshawar Museum, home to one of the great Gandhara collections. Islamia College, with its distinctive Indo-Saracenic silhouette, anchors the city's education identity.

Peshawar's name travels on two exports above all: the Peshawari chappal, and the chapli kebab, the flat, tomato-topped kebab of the province.
