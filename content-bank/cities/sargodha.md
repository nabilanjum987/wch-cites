## City intro

Sargodha, in Punjab, counted 872,557 residents at the 2017 census. It carries two titles at once: the City of Eagles, for its Pakistan Air Force identity — PAF Base Mushaf stands here — and the citrus capital of Pakistan, for the kinnow orchards of the surrounding district.

The city was planned in the canal-colony era and it shows: the Fifteen Bazaars give its centre a numbered-grid legibility, the Kirana Hills rise at its edge, and the University of Sargodha anchors its education sector.

Sargodha's seasons are measured in citrus. When the kinnow harvest comes in, packing houses and juice plants — the Shezan name among them — run at full stretch, and the district's fruit moves out across Pakistan and to export markets.
