## City intro

New York City is the largest city of the United States — about 8,804,190 people (2020 census figure, as packaged from Wikidata) in the America/New_York time zone. Its symbols are the packaged landmark set: the Statue of Liberty in the harbour, the Empire State Building, Central Park's green rectangle, Times Square, and the Brooklyn Bridge over the East River.

The city is five boroughs on three islands and the mainland, and its identity is arrival: generations entered America through this harbour, and the city still runs on the energy of people coming to make something.

New York is not the US capital — Washington, D.C. is — but it is the country's financial, media and cultural centre, and the seat of the United Nations.

## Economy

New York is the centre of US finance — Wall Street and the country's largest stock exchanges sit here. ## Living & practical

New York has hot summers and cold winters; the live weather layer on the city page carries current conditions from the National Weather Service. The currency is the US dollar (USD). The packaged food list is the city's own canon: New York-style pizza, the bagel, New York cheesecake, the pastrami sandwich and the hot dog.
