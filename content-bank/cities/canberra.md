## City intro

Canberra is the capital of Australia — about 473,855 people (2024, as packaged from Wikidata) in the Australia/Sydney time zone — a planned city built in the twentieth century for the single purpose of being a capital. Its geometry is deliberate: Parliament House on its hill, the Australian War Memorial facing it across Lake Burley Griffin, and the National Gallery of Australia within the parliamentary triangle. Old Parliament House, the capital's seat for most of the century, stands preserved below its successor.

Canberra's register is garden-city: low density, bushland ridges inside the city boundary, and a civic calm that distinguishes it from every other Australian city in this bank.
