## City intro

Doha is the capital of Qatar — a city of about 1,186,023 people (2020, as packaged from Wikidata) on a bay of the Gulf, in the Asia/Qatar time zone. In a generation it has been rebuilt around landmark culture: the Museum of Islamic Art on its own island in the bay, the National Museum of Qatar wrapped around the old palace, and Katara Cultural Village along the shore.

The old Doha survives inside the new: Souq Waqif, the restored market of alleys, falcon shops and cafés, is the city's social heart, and the Pearl Island extends the city into the sea on reclaimed land.

Doha is the seat of Qatar's government and the hub of an economy built on liquefied natural gas — and, since the football World Cup it hosted, one of the most recognisable city skylines in the Gulf.

## Economy

The packaged national frame (World Bank): Qatar's GDP was US$215.6 billion in 2025, with GDP per capita of US$72,525 — among the highest in the world — unemployment of 0.13% (2025), inflation of 1.27% (2024) and life expectancy of 82.5 years. Doha concentrates the state, energy and finance functions behind those figures. The package's local sources are the Qatar Planning and Statistics Authority and the Qatar Open Data Portal. No city-level GDP figure is claimed: the package carries none. (A packaged area figure for Doha was found corrupt at ingest and is deliberately not used anywhere in this bank.)

## Living & practical

Doha's summers are extremely hot; the live weather layer on the city page is essential planning input, and the city's public life shifts outdoors in the cooler months and the evenings. The currency is the Qatari riyal (QAR); Arabic is the official language, with English widely used.

The packaged food list: machboos, harees, luqaimat, balaleet and thareed — the shared Gulf table, with machboos the national centrepiece.

Emergency number (packaged): 999 for police, ambulance and fire.
