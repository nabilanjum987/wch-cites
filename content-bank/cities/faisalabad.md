## City intro

Faisalabad is Pakistan's textile capital — the "Manchester of Pakistan" — and one of the country's largest cities, with 3,203,846 residents at the 2017 census. It sits in central Punjab at 184 metres, planned around one of the most recognisable street patterns in the country: eight bazaars radiating from a central clock tower, laid out in the pattern of the Union Jack.

Founded as Lyallpur in the canal-colony era, the city grew as an agricultural market town before industry took over. The Clock Tower and its Eight Bazaars remain the civic centre, alongside the Lyallpur Museum, Jinnah Garden and the Gumti Water Fountain.

Faisalabad's cultural exports match its industrial ones: it is the home city of Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, whose name is inseparable from modern qawwali, and of his nephew and successor Rahat Fateh Ali Khan.
