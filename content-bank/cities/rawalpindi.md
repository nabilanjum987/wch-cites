## City intro

Rawalpindi, in northern Punjab, counted 2,098,231 residents at the 2017 census. At 508 metres on the Potohar plateau, it is the twin city of Islamabad — the older, denser half of the capital region, with historic bazaars where the planned capital's grid gives way to centuries of market streets. Raja Bazaar is its commercial heart.

The city's public life centres on Liaquat Bagh, its great public ground, and its green escapes at Ayub National Park and Rawal Lake on the road toward Islamabad. The Kartarpura food street carries its culinary name — breakfast there is an institution of the twin cities.

Rawalpindi is also a garrison and market city in equal measure: leather goods, pottery and textiles move through its wholesale bazaars, serving both the capital region and the routes north.
