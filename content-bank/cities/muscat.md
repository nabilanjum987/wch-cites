## City intro

Muscat is the capital of Oman — about 1,421,409 people (2019, as packaged from Wikidata) in the Asia/Muscat time zone — a low, white city strung along a rocky Gulf of Oman coast, deliberately unlike the tower capitals around it. Its crown is the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, and its stage set is the Muttrah Corniche, where the port, the souk and the mountains meet.

The Al Alam Palace, the Sultan's ceremonial residence, sits in old Muscat between its two Portuguese-era forts; the Royal Opera House Muscat gives the capital its modern cultural landmark. Muscat's register is maritime and measured — a trading port of frankincense-era lineage that still faces the sea.

Muscat concentrates the Sultanate's government, port and finance functions. ## Living & practical

Muscat is hot for most of the year, with milder winters; the live weather layer on the city page carries current conditions. The currency is the Omani rial (OMR). The packaged food list is Omani: shuwa (slow-cooked marinated meat), mashuai, harees, halwa and laban.
