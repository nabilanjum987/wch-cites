## City intro

George Town is the capital of Penang, Malaysia — about 794,313 people (2020, as packaged from Wikidata) in the Asia/Kuala_Lumpur time zone. Its historic core is one of Asia's great preserved port cities: clan jetties stretching into the strait, the Khoo Kongsi clan house, the blue Cheong Fatt Tze Mansion, and shophouses in every style the port's centuries produced.

Above it rise the city's two green landmarks: Penang Hill and the Kek Lok Si temple complex, the largest Buddhist temple in Malaysia. George Town's other fame is edible — it is routinely counted among Asia's great street-food cities, and its packaged food list is the evidence.
