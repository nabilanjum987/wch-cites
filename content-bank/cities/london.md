## City intro

London is the capital of the United Kingdom — about 8,799,728 people (2021, as packaged from Wikidata) in the Europe/London time zone. It is a city built in rings of history around the Thames: the Tower of London guarding the old river gate, the great clock tower known worldwide as Big Ben at Westminster, Buckingham Palace, and the British Museum's collections at Bloomsbury.

The London Eye turns above the South Bank; the financial districts at the City and Canary Wharf give the skyline its second centre. London's scale is metropolitan rather than monumental — a federation of boroughs and neighbourhoods that happens to be one city.

Its population is one of the most international in the world, and its institutions — Parliament, the monarchy, the City markets — make it a capital in every sense the word carries.
