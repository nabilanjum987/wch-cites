## City intro

Sharjah is the third city of the United Arab Emirates — about 1,405,843 people (2015, as packaged from Wikidata) in the Asia/Dubai time zone — and the federation's cultural capital. Its identity is curated heritage: the Heart of Sharjah restoration district, the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization, the Al Noor Mosque on the Khalid Lagoon corniche, and the Sharjah Art Foundation's spaces in the old merchants' houses.

Al Majaz Waterfront gives the city its family promenade. Where Dubai is vertical and commercial, Sharjah is horizontal and scholarly — the emirate of museums, book fairs and restored souks, sharing a continuous urban fabric with Dubai while keeping a distinctly quieter register.
