# Pisces — Sign Profile (Evergreen)

*For entertainment: this profile is written in the tradition of popular astrology and is presented for enjoyment. It is not a factual or predictive claim about any person.*

**Dates:** 19 February – 20 March · **Element:** Water · **Mode:** Mutable · **Ruler in the site's system:** Neptune (traditional ruler: Jupiter)

## The Pisces character

Pisces is the two fish, swimming in opposite directions — the sign of dreams, compassion and porous boundaries, and the last sign of the zodiac. In the popular portrait, Pisces people feel the weather of every room: empathic to the point of absorption, imaginative, gentle, and happiest near water, music or sleep. In the site's computed system the sign is read through Neptune, the modern ruler of dreams and dissolution (its traditional ruler is Jupiter). The mutable water shows as adaptability of feeling — Pisces becomes, a little, whoever they are with.

The shadow side in the tradition is the same openness unguarded: escapism, vagueness about facts and money, and other people's sorrows carried as their own.

## Strengths

Compassion is the headline: the folklore's Pisces is the friend who understands without being told. Add creativity that arrives as atmosphere — music, image, story — intuition that bypasses evidence and is often right, and a forgiveness that resets relationships others would have ended.

## Career

The Pisces profile suits work where feeling is the instrument: the arts and music, film, healing and therapy, nursing, charity and spiritual vocations, photography and design — and any role needing imagination in the room. In the folklore, Pisces frays in rigid, metric-only environments and flourishes where the work has meaning and the schedule has some tide in it.

## Relationships

In love, the tradition paints Pisces as the romantic idealist: devotion with a soundtrack, love as merging rather than meeting. Courtship in the folklore is tender and creative; Pisces falls for kindness and for wounded beauty alike. Friction points: boundaries dissolving (rescue fantasies, difficulty saying no), reality edited to protect the dream, and hurt expressed as disappearance rather than argument. Compatible portraits in the tradition are the fellow water signs (Cancer, Scorpio) and the earth signs (Taurus, Capricorn) who offer the shore.

## The Pisces note

The profile's lesson, in its own tradition, is the shoreline: the fish need the sea — Pisces at its best keeps the dream and learns where the water ends.
