# Libra — Sign Profile (Evergreen)

*For entertainment: this profile is written in the tradition of popular astrology and is presented for enjoyment. It is not a factual or predictive claim about any person.*

**Dates:** 23 September – 22 October · **Element:** Air · **Mode:** Cardinal · **Traditional ruler:** Venus

## The Libra character

Libra is the scales — the sign of balance, beauty and the other person. In the popular portrait, Libra people are diplomats by instinct: they hear both sides, smooth the room, and cannot rest while things are unfair or ugly. Venus rules here as it does in Taurus, but in air rather than earth: Libra's Venus is social and aesthetic — harmony between people, proportion in things. The cardinal mode hides inside the charm: Libra initiates — relationships, projects, peace processes.

The shadow side in the tradition is the balancing act itself: indecision, people-pleasing, and conflict avoided until it explodes.

## Strengths

Fairness is the headline: the folklore's Libra is the friend both sides call. Add charm that is really attentiveness, taste, negotiation skill, and a genuine horror of needless cruelty or mess.

## Career

The Libra profile suits work between people: law and mediation, diplomacy and human resources, design and fashion, public relations, counselling, and any partnership business. In the folklore, Libra produces their best work in collaboration and in beautiful order, and quietly resents environments that are brutal, drab or unjust.

## Relationships

In love, the tradition paints Libra as the romantic of the zodiac: partnership is their native country, and courtship is an art form — setting, words, gesture. The folklore's Libra is rarely happily single for long. Friction points: indecision that partners experience as distance, peace kept at the price of honesty, and the need for a relationship to look right as well as feel right. Compatible portraits in the tradition are the fellow air signs (Gemini, Aquarius) and the fire signs (Leo, Sagittarius) who bring the warmth Libra admires.

## The Libra note

The profile's lesson, in its own tradition, is decision: balance is not the refusal to choose — it is choosing with both sides honestly weighed, then standing still no longer.
