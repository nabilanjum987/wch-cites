# Scorpio — Sign Profile (Evergreen)

*For entertainment: this profile is written in the tradition of popular astrology and is presented for enjoyment. It is not a factual or predictive claim about any person.*

**Dates:** 23 October – 21 November · **Element:** Water · **Mode:** Fixed · **Traditional ruler:** Mars (in the site's system; modern astrology also assigns Pluto — see the framework note)

## The Scorpio character

Scorpio is the zodiac's depth charge. In the popular portrait, Scorpio people run intense and private: they feel everything, reveal selectively, and have an instinct for the truth under the surface — the motive, the secret, the real story. The water element shows as emotional power rather than softness; the fixed mode as will. In the site's computed system, Scorpio is read through Mars, its traditional ruler — drive, desire and conflict — with Pluto deliberately omitted from the calculations (see the monthly framework file).

The shadow side in the tradition matches the depth: jealousy, secrecy turned to control, and a sting kept for betrayals — Scorpio in folklore neither forgets nor entirely forgives.

## Strengths

Intensity, aimed, is the headline strength: focus that borders on obsession, loyalty that survives storms, courage in dark subjects others avoid — death, debt, desire, crisis — and a detective's patience for the truth.

## Career

The Scorpio profile suits work at the hidden layer: investigation and research, medicine and surgery, psychology, finance and insurance, intelligence and security, crisis management, and any craft dealing with other people's secrets or transformations. In the folklore, Scorpio needs meaningful stakes and real autonomy; trivial work under watchful managers is the combination they walk away from.

## Relationships

In love, the tradition paints Scorpio as total: all-or-nothing devotion, physical and emotional intensity, and a demand for honesty as the price of the famous loyalty. Courtship is magnetic and testing — Scorpio watches whether a partner can be trusted with the unedited self. Friction points: jealousy and possessiveness, power struggles inside intimacy, and feelings too deep for casual expression, which partners can misread as coldness. Compatible portraits in the tradition are the fellow water signs (Cancer, Pisces) and the earth signs (Virgo, Capricorn) with the steadiness to match the depth.

## The Scorpio note

The profile's lesson, in its own tradition, is trust: the phoenix is Scorpio's other symbol — the power is not in holding the ashes but in rising from them.
