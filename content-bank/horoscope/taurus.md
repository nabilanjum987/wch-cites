# Taurus — Sign Profile (Evergreen)

*For entertainment: this profile is written in the tradition of popular astrology and is presented for enjoyment. It is not a factual or predictive claim about any person.*

**Dates:** 20 April – 20 May · **Element:** Earth · **Mode:** Fixed · **Traditional ruler:** Venus

## The Taurus character

Taurus is the zodiac's builder. In the popular portrait, Taurus people are steady, sensual and stubborn in the best sense: they build slowly, choose carefully, and then hold. The earth element shows as practicality — a preference for what can be touched, tasted and kept — and Venus, the ruler, adds the aesthetic streak: good food, good fabric, good music are not luxuries to Taurus but necessities of a life properly lived.

The shadow side in the tradition is the fixed mode dug in: resistance to change even when change is overdue, possessiveness, and a temper that is slow to rise and slow to fall.

## Strengths

Reliability is the headline: the Taurus promise, once given, is the folklore's gold standard. Add patience, loyalty, an eye for quality and value, and a calm that steadies other people in a panic. Taurus in the tradition finishes what others start.

## Career

The Taurus profile suits work where quality compounds: finance and banking, farming and food, building and craft, design, beauty and the luxury trades — Venus's territory — and any role where a steady hand on long projects wins. In the folklore, Taurus dislikes being rushed or reorganised mid-task, and produces their best work with clear terms, fair pay and time to do it properly.

## Relationships

In love, the tradition paints Taurus as devoted and tactile: love expressed in meals, gifts, presence and touch rather than speeches. Courtship is unhurried and, once committed, the folklore's Taurus is in for the long term. Friction points: jealousy, a need for reassurance that can read as possession, and difficulty letting go even when a relationship has run its course. Compatible portraits in the tradition are the fellow earth signs (Virgo, Capricorn) and the water signs (Cancer, Pisces).

## The Taurus note

The profile's lesson, in its own tradition, is release: the bull's strength is holding on — its growth is learning what is no longer worth holding.
