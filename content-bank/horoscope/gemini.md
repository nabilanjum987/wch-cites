# Gemini — Sign Profile (Evergreen)

*For entertainment: this profile is written in the tradition of popular astrology and is presented for enjoyment. It is not a factual or predictive claim about any person.*

**Dates:** 21 May – 20 June · **Element:** Air · **Mode:** Mutable · **Traditional ruler:** Mercury

## The Gemini character

Gemini is the twins — the sign of two minds, and the popular portrait leans into it: curious, quick, verbal and versatile, Gemini people collect facts, people and projects the way other signs collect objects. The air element shows as thought and talk; Mercury, the ruler, is the messenger planet, and Gemini in the folklore is the zodiac's communicator — the one who knows a little about everything and exactly who to call for the rest.

The shadow side is the flip of versatility: restlessness, scattered focus, and a boredom threshold so low it shapes life choices. The tradition's Gemini would rather be interested than settled.

## Strengths

Adaptability is the headline: Gemini adjusts mid-sentence. Add wit, learning speed, social fluency — the folklore's Gemini can talk to anyone — and a youthful curiosity that does not age on schedule.

## Career

The Gemini profile suits work made of words, ideas and people: journalism, writing, teaching, sales, public relations, broadcasting, translation and any trade where information moves. Two projects at once is not a burden in the folklore but a preference; the enemy is the single repetitive task. Variety is the working condition Gemini negotiates for, consciously or not.

## Relationships

In love, the tradition paints Gemini as playful and mentally hungry: conversation is the courtship, and a partner who cannot talk will not hold them. They fall for minds first. Friction points: inconsistency that partners read as unreliability, a need for novelty, and feelings processed by talking about them at length — or deflected with a joke. Compatible portraits in the tradition are the fellow air signs (Libra, Aquarius) and the fire signs (Aries, Leo) that keep pace.

## The Gemini note

The profile's lesson, in its own tradition, is depth: the twins' gift is range — their growth is staying with one thing long enough to master it.
