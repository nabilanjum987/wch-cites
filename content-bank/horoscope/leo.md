# Leo — Sign Profile (Evergreen)

*For entertainment: this profile is written in the tradition of popular astrology and is presented for enjoyment. It is not a factual or predictive claim about any person.*

**Dates:** 23 July – 22 August · **Element:** Fire · **Mode:** Fixed · **Traditional ruler:** The Sun

## The Leo character

Leo is the lion, ruled by the Sun itself, and the popular portrait is solar: warm, radiant and built for the centre of the room. In the folklore, Leo people are performers by nature — generous hosts, natural storytellers, people whose confidence is less arrogance than a genuine belief that life is a stage and they intend to give a good show. The fire element shows as enthusiasm and drama; the fixed mode adds the pride and the loyalty underneath.

The shadow side is the same sun, eclipsed: a hunger for applause that can curdle into vanity, pride that cannot apologise, and generosity with an audience expected.

## Strengths

Generosity is the headline — the tradition's Leo gives big: praise, protection, celebration. Add courage, charisma, creative flair, and a warmth that makes other people feel like somebody. Leo in the folklore defends friends like territory.

## Career

The Leo profile suits work with an audience and a spotlight: acting and entertainment, leadership and management, teaching, politics, branding and luxury, events — anywhere presence is the product. In the folklore, Leo needs recognition the way other signs need salary, and a wise employer pays in both. Routine invisibility is the condition Leo quietly quits.

## Relationships

In love, the tradition paints Leo as grand romance: courtship with ceremony, gifts, devotion declared out loud. Leo loves being adored and, in the generous version of the folklore, adores right back. Friction points: pride in arguments (winning can cost the relationship), a need for admiration that partners must not starve, and jealousy when the spotlight moves. Compatible portraits in the tradition are the fellow fire signs (Aries, Sagittarius) and the air signs (Gemini, Libra) that applaud and sparkle back.

## The Leo note

The profile's lesson, in its own tradition, is humility: the lion is king by nature, not by announcement — Leo at its best shines and lets others shine in the same light.
