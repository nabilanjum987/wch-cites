## Narration script
Welcome to the Horoscope hub of WorldCityHub — twelve signs, one page each, and a daily habit we intend to earn honestly.

Let us begin with the honesty, because this corner of the internet rarely does. A horoscope is entertainment built on an ancient symbolic system; it is not a measurement like the weather on our city pages, and this site will not dress it as one. What we can promise is craft: a page for every sign, written to the standard of the big astrology apps, updated on a daily rhythm, and — where the hub shows the machinery of the craft — computed rather than invented.

That machinery matters. The sky is real, and its positions can be calculated exactly. The direction this hub is being built takes the per-sign pages beyond generic rotating text: the transits behind each reading are worked out with genuine astronomy mathematics and labelled as computed; the traditional furniture of a daily reading — a lucky colour, a lucky number, a favourable time, the day's energy read across love, work, and wellbeing — is derived from that day's configuration rather than pasted from a stock paragraph written for nobody in particular. No page here will pretend a computer has peered into your soul. It will show you the day's pattern for your sign, cleanly made, and leave the meaning to you — which is, historically, what horoscopes were always for.

Why does a city-data site keep a horoscope hub at all? Because habits build audiences, and audiences return. The traveller checks a city's weather once; the reader who opens her sign with morning tea returns three hundred mornings a year — and some of those mornings, she also checks the gold price, her city's prayer times, and the news. The handover behind this site calls horoscope a priority cluster for exactly that reason, and the hub is kept to the same hygiene as everything else here: no psychic upsells, no paid readings, no frightening predictions written to sell a remedy.

Find your sign on the page below — all twelve are there, one tap each — and make it a small daily ritual if it pleases you. Read it the way its makers intend: as a well-made moment of pattern and possibility at the start of the day, honestly labelled, freely given.

A final word about the calendar of the thing. The hub runs on the date where you are, and each sign's page turns over with the day — which makes it a small, reliable appointment in a medium of endless scroll. Readers of the big astrology apps describe the same ritual: coffee, the day's reading, a breath before the noise. This hub asks for that minute and promises, in exchange, no alarm, no salesmanship, and no recycled paragraphs wearing today's date as a disguise. Twelve signs, twelve pages, one honest habit — rebuilt daily, like the rest of this site. And the hub keeps one more promise the big apps often break: your sign is free, all of it, every day. The full reading is the reading — not a teaser behind a paywall, not a first paragraph with the meaning locked in a subscription. Whatever this hub becomes, that term does not change.
