## Narration script
This is the Atlantic Ocean — the second largest of the world's five oceans, and the one that history crossed first.

The measured facts, from the sources our page cites. The Atlantic covers about eighty-five million square kilometres — roughly seventeen percent of the Earth's surface and about twenty-four percent of its water. It holds the Americas to its west and Europe and Africa — the Old World, as the age of sail named Afro-Eurasia — to its east, and during the Age of Discovery it was precisely that separation which defined it: the wide water between the worlds that ships were learning to cross. Down its centre runs the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, the longest mountain range on the planet, mostly hidden beneath the waves, where the seafloor itself is still spreading and the ocean is, measurably, growing wider year by year.
