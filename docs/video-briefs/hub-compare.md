## Narration script
Welcome to Compare — the part of WorldCityHub where two places stand side by side, answer the same questions, and accept the verdict.

The idea is simple, and the execution is strict. Pick a pair — Lahore against Karachi, Karachi against Islamabad, and the other published pairings — and the page measures both cities with the same instruments at the same moment: the live weather, the air quality, the day's prayer times, the facts of population and place. One template serves every pairing, so a comparison can never be rigged by changing the questions between cities. What one side is asked, the other side is asked. Then, under the measurements, a verdict line says plainly which city leads on the evidence — and on another day, with another day's weather in it, the verdict is free to change.

Here is what you will not find, and the absence is deliberate. Many comparison sites will happily score cities on cost of living, safety, and salaries — numbers that look precise and are, too often, stitched together from stale surveys and anonymous guesses. We looked for honest, free, checkable sources for those categories and did not find them, so those rows do not appear. The scorecard shows what we can measure, and stays silent where we cannot. A comparison with three true rows teaches you more than one with ten decorative ones.

The pairs themselves are chosen by demand, not by the size of our database: the rivalries people actually argue about. Lahore and Karachi — the cultural capital against the port metropolis. Karachi and Islamabad — the giant against the planned capital. Each pair gets a permanent page, so the argument can be settled, or at least restarted, with today's readings in hand.

Use it before a move, before a trip, or before winning an argument at dinner. And use it in the spirit it was built: the figures are live, the template is even-handed, and the verdict belongs to the evidence of the day — not to the city's reputation, and not to ours.

The pairs are waiting at the link below. Choose yours, and may the better city — today — win.

There is a deeper reason this hub exists, and it sits at the centre of the whole site. WorldCityHub is built on templates: the same page, honestly filled, for every city. Compare is where that philosophy becomes visible — because only when every city has been measured with the same instruments can two of them stand side by side without the comparison itself becoming a kind of favouritism. As more cities pass the quality gate and join the published set, more pairs become possible, and the hub grows the way the site grows: by verification, not by enthusiasm. If the pair you want is not here yet, it is not forgotten; it is waiting its turn behind the same gate. One more use deserves naming: settlement. Families divided between two cities — and in this audience, that is half the families we know — argue from memory and loyalty. This hub gives them a shared scoreboard: not a winner for all time, but a fair reading for today, taken with the same instruments on both sides of the argument. It will not end the argument. It will improve it.
