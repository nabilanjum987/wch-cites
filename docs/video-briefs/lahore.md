## Narration script
Welcome to Lahore — the cultural capital of Pakistan, and the city where WorldCityHub's live city guide began.

Lahore is the capital of Punjab and home to just over thirteen million people. It stands in the northeast of Pakistan, close to the Indian border, at 31.52 degrees north and 74.36 degrees east — on the historic road between Delhi and Kabul, a position that made it for centuries one of the great crossroads cities of South Asia. Empires understood its value: whoever held Lahore held the gateway to Hindustan.

The city's heart is the Walled City, and above its gate rises the Badshahi Mosque. Completed in 1673 under the Mughal emperor Aurangzeb, it was for centuries the largest mosque in the world. Its vast red-sandstone courtyard, its white marble domes, and the Alamgiri Gate at its steps still set the tone for everything around them. Directly opposite stands Lahore Fort — the citadel rebuilt by emperors in marble and mirror-work — and between mosque and fort, the old city unrolls in bazaars, havelis, and food streets that have fed travellers for four hundred years.

But Lahore is not a museum piece. It is a working megacity of universities and courts, publishers and film studios, tech startups and textile mills. It is the city Pakistanis point to when they argue — and they argue happily — about where the country's soul lives. The old saying claims that whoever has not seen Lahore has not been born. Lahoris quote it often, and they mean it without a trace of irony.
