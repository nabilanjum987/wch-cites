# Social Plan — WorldCityHub (PLANS ONLY)

**Status:** PLAN ONLY (Builder Batch 4, Item 4b — 2026-10-23). No account
has been created, nothing posted, no automation connected. The owner
creates every account himself, on his own phone; the agent creates
nothing.

**Principle:** social exists to send people to pages that are already
live and true. Every figure a post quotes is pulled from the site's own
live page on the day of posting — a post never carries a number that
isn't on the page it links to, with the page named as the source.

## Posting rules (hard)

1. **No automation.** No schedulers, bots, auto-posters, or API posting.
   Every post is made by a human hand, in the app, at posting time.
2. **No spam patterns.** No mass-following, follow/unfollow cycles,
   comment pods, hashtag stuffing, copy-paste replies, or posting the
   same text to groups/communities that didn't ask for it.
3. **One account per platform.** No alts, no duplicate pages, no
   persona accounts.
4. **No bought anything.** No followers, likes, views, or engagement —
   bought numbers are fraud against the next sponsor/partner who reads
   them, and they poison the analytics the site plans by.
5. **Cadence caps.** Maximum one anchor post per platform per day (the
   daily figure clip counts as that post on clip days). Rest days in
   the calendar are deliberate.
6. **Traceability.** Every claim and figure in a post must trace to a
   live page figure pulled that day, with the page linked in the post.
   If it isn't on the site, it isn't in the post.
7. **Corrections.** A wrong figure gets a visible correction post (not
   a silent edit-and-hope), and the source page is checked first — if
   the page is wrong, the page is fixed before the post is.
8. **English only** on all public posts (the site's language policy),
   and no commenting on politics/religion beyond what the linked page
   itself states as fact.
