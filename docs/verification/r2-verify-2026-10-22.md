## Findings (defect list)

**No code defects were found in the R/batch-2 outputs by this pass.**
The following are observations and already-disclosed items, restated so
billi has them in one place:

1. **Migration numbering gap at 0010.** The union sequence runs
   0001–0009, then 0011–0013 (my coordinator-assigned numbers). If no
   other stream owns 0010, the gap is harmless (Supabase applies by
   filename order) — flagged so the coordinator can confirm ownership
   before the owner applies the stack.
2. **R9's open root cause stands**: `getMockCityData` fabricates stub
   cities for unresolved paths (wrong-province URLs render instead of
   404ing). Documented in PR #49 with a recommended data-layer card;
   it is the single largest remaining link-integrity item and was
   deliberately not patched inside the audit card's remit.
3. **R10's structural recommendations stand** (PR #50): country/rates
   client-monolith splitting and the next/image migration are the real
   performance wins; the attribute fixes shipped there do not move
   bundle weight and were reported as such.
4. **Thin-but-disclosed profiles**: Nasir Hussain Shah (year-precision
   birth, 2 timeline entries) and Haider Zaman Khan (birth year only)
   carry their precision limits inside their own summaries (PR #54).
5. **Events coverage for the new cities is 1 row** (Cholistan Rally,
   Bahawalpur). Sargodha, Abbottabad, Sukkur and Mardan have no
   verifiable dated city editions yet — disclosed in PR #54 rather
   than filled with inventions.
6. **Sagrada Família timeline wording** references the 2026 centenary
   programme in present tense — accurate at writing; it will read as
   history after 2026 and needs no action now.
7. **R4B package errata already corrected downstream**: Mir Muhammad
   Masum's birth year (package seed 1576 → Wikidata 1538, corrected in
   the PR #54 pool profile with a note) and the Bahawalpur
   feet-as-metres elevation trap (118 m used; trap documented in the
   package itself).
